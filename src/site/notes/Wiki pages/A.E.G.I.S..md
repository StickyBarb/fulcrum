The **Alliance for Extraterrestrial Geostrategic Intelligence and Security (A.E.G.I.S.)** is a multinational security and intelligence agency formed in response to the confirmed threat of extraterrestrial incursions. Created under United Nations oversight following the [[Wiki pages/Chelyabinsk invasion\|Chelyabinsk invasion]], A.E.G.I.S. serves as Earth’s foremost global defense initiative against alien threats and metahuman destabilization.

## Background and Formation

In the aftermath of the Chelyabinsk incident, in which Russian forces attempted and failed to contain an incursion by the alien force now identified as the Serebreyaki, classified information and footage from the event leaked online. These included videos of the invasion and photographs of cryptic messages left deep in Russia’s underground tunnels. The images and recordings sparked an international media frenzy and exposed humanity’s vulnerability to off-world forces.

Named after the Russian word _srebro_ (сребро), meaning "silver," the Serebryaki were so dubbed due to the reflective metallic sheen of their drone units. Their brief but devastating emergence prompted immediate diplomatic summits and crisis management meetings across the globe.

Soon after, the United Nations passed a resolution to establish a neutral, internationally governed agency that could respond decisively to extraterrestrial threats. A collaborative military, scientific, and intelligence agency free from singular national allegiance was born, named the **Alliance for Extraterrestrial Geostrategic Intelligence and Security (A.E.G.I.S.)**.

## Mandate and Mission

A.E.G.I.S. operates with two primary directives:

1. **Extraterrestrial Defense:** Investigate, assess, and respond to extraterrestrial incursions, signals, technologies, or related phenomena.
2. **Global Security:** Monitor and mitigate internal threats to Earth’s planetary integrity, including rogue metahumans, experimental technology, and alien-influenced factions.

## Structure and Operations

A.E.G.I.S. functions as a coordinated network of research facilities, forward bases, satellite stations, and diplomatic envoys. It is known to partner with national militaries, universities, aerospace firms, and independent metahuman teams. Its highest-ranking officials report directly to a multinational Security Council composed of rotating delegates from member states.

## Public Perception

A.E.G.I.S. is generally viewed as a necessary evolution of planetary defense, though it has come under scrutiny for its secrecy and occasional jurisdictional overreach. Critics argue that its surveillance capabilities and power to detain metahumans without trial present ethical challenges. Nevertheless, its role in preventing further incursions and responding to alien threats has maintained its global credibility.
